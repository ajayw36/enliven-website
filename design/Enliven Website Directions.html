Our mission

Inter­generational connection makes a vibrant community.

Youth share their musical talents and cultural perspectives to entertain and uplift. Elders share their love, wisdom and support. Both leave with something they didn't have before.

photo · performance, wide
Est.
2023
Founded by students in Newton, MA. Now across Greater Boston.

How a performance comes together

01

A community reaches out

Tell us about your room and residents. We'll suggest a format — a concert, a dance showcase, a hands-on art hour.

02

Students sign up

Volunteers pick a date, rehearse a short set and prepare a few words to introduce each piece and its tradition.

03

We share an afternoon

Performance, then conversation. The time after the last note is often the part everyone remembers.

Want to be part of it?

Students, performers, senior communities, families — one form for everyone. We reply within a few days.

1Enliven website — three directions, varying on mood
Context & assumptions
Palette is pulled straight from the logo: cream paper, near-black ink, one coral and one gold. Nothing else — this is the fix for the clashing colors. Logo unchanged. Two audiences get equal billing (centers / students); student sign-up links out to the existing Google Form. Photos and videos are placeholders until you share real media.
What's the same across all three
Pages: Home, About, Gallery, Testimonials (contact form for centers lives at the bottom of About and in the footer). Stats: 1,000+ seniors, 50+ students, 30+ events. Surgeon General quote on Home. Partners: Sunrise of Newton, Fox Hill Village, North Hill. Copy tone is warm and heartfelt.
How they differ
1a Warm & joyful — rounded arches, sun-ray motif from the logo, both accents used freely.
1b Calm & editorial — serif, hairline gold rules, coral only on actions.
1c Bold & youthful — geometric caps echoing the wordmark, black sections, big gold numerals.
1aWarm & joyful · Outfit + Nunito Sans · cream / coral / gold
